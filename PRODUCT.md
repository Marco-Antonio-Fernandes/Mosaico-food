# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Store owners and staff operating a Brazilian snack bar. The desktop is used at the counter throughout service to track orders, stock, customers and receipts. Context inferred from the README, the existing app and the cash workflow agreed with the user.

## Product Purpose

Keep counter, WhatsApp and assistant sales consistent. Let staff trace every receipt, count physical cash, close a session and consult its history.

## Brand Personality

Practical, clear and reliable. Interface text is in Brazilian Portuguese. Explain operational states and the action needed in familiar store language.

## Anti-references

Avoid decorative financial dashboards, invented balances and modal-heavy workflows. Demonstrative numbers must never appear as live financial data.

## Design Principles

Use the existing navigation and components. Put the current task first. Identify the source of every movement. Separate physical cash from digital receipts. Keep historical views read-only and show recoverable errors without erasing form inputs.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Visible labels, keyboard operation, meaningful focus, textual status alongside color, responsive tables and reduced motion. No additional accessibility standard has been specified by the user.
